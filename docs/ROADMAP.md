# Development Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Core foundation

Organization/tenancy, users/roles, departments, locations, vendors, categories/products, PO/GRN foundation, asset registry, QR IDs, movements, dashboard, seed data and audit foundation.

## Phase 2 — Procurement and stores

Purchase requisitions, configurable approvals, RFQ/tender, quotation comparison, PO approval, GRN screens, inspection, warehouses/bins, material batches, stock ledger, issue/return notes and reorder levels.

## Phase 3 — Asset operations

Custodian assignment, transfer approvals, maintenance tickets, preventive schedules, AMC, calibration, warranty/insurance, document uploads, notifications, disposal and depreciation integration.

## Phase 4 — Mobile verification

PWA or Flutter application, QR/barcode scanner, offline-first field verification, GPS capture where policy permits, photo evidence and discrepancy workflow.

## Phase 5 — Enterprise/government hardening

SSO/MFA, capability permissions, workflow designer, immutable audit exports, security testing, DR, HA, observability, retention controls, accessibility, localization and integration adapters.

## Phase 6 — Analytics/AI

OCR/document extraction, PO-invoice-GRN matching assistance, vendor-risk indicators, abnormal procurement detection, natural-language reporting and predictive maintenance experiments with human approval.
